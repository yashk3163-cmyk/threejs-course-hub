## Notable findings during the build

- Three.js r186 silently ignores space-separated `rgb(255 136 0)` colours (no warning, colour unchanged). C02's config check rejects them with a message; a unit test compares the check with Three.js's actual behaviour.
- On a phone-shaped window the knot was cut off at both sides. Fixed: the camera steps back on tall screens (`src/framing.js`).
- The browser asked for a missing `favicon.ico`, which showed as a Console error. Fixed with an inline icon, so "no red errors in the Console" is a reliable success signal for learners.
